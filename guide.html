SYSTEM INSTRUCTIONS

HOW TO PLAY

1. GOAL: Match the exact code sequence shown under HAUNT SEQUENCE into your buffer before time runs out.

2. MOVES: Your first click MUST be from ROW 1. After that, your picks MUST alternate between the SAME COLUMN and SAME ROW of your last pick.

3. BUFFER LIMIT: You only have 4 Buffer Slots. Fill all 4 slots without completing the sequence and system locks you out.

4. Point System: points you receive are equal to remaining time mutiplied by 100.
